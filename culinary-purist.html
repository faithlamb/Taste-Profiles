The Culinary Purist

Technique, quality, and chef-driven excellence are what you care about most. You’re paying attention to every bite: the balance of flavors, the precision, the way a dish is plated and executed. You appreciate that food is a craft. You’re drawn to tasting menus, seasonal ingredients, and restaurants where the chef’s vision really comes through. You’d rather have one incredible, perfectly executed dish than ten average ones, and you’re always looking for the place that does it best.
