The Social Foodie

For you, food is all about connection. Who you share with is just as important as the food you enjoy. You gravitate toward places made for group dinners, where plates are meant to be shared so everyone gets a taste of everything. You love lively, energetic atmospheres buzzing with conversation and joy. Whether it’s a long dinner that turns into hours of talking or a spontaneous night out with friends, you’re there for the shared experience. The best meals, in your opinion, are the ones you don’t eat alone.
