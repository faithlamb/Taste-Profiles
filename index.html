Introduction

This project explores how personalized taste profiles can enhance the user experience of restaurant discovery tools by combining curated food personas with interactive geospatial visualization. While many recommendation platforms rely on generic popularity metrics or algorithmic suggestions, our approach centers the user’s individual dining identity as the primary driver of discovery. By mapping restaurant recommendations across major U.S. foodie hubs (Miami, New York, Dallas, Las Vegas, and Los Angeles) we demonstrate how taste‑based personalization can create a more intuitive, engaging, and meaningful way to explore dining options. The site presents six distinct taste profiles, each represented by a dedicated interactive map built in Mapbox. These maps visualize curated restaurant selections tailored to specific dining identities, from the Trend Chaser to the Comfort Seeker. Together, the profiles and maps illustrate how personalized frameworks can bridge the gap between user preference and spatial exploration, offering a more human‑centered alternative to traditional restaurant recommendation systems.
