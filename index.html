Introduction

Take this quiz to find your personalized taste profiles. It enhances the chore of using mundane restaurant discovery tools by combining curated food personas with interactive geospatial visualization. Many recommendation platforms rely on generic popularity metrics or algorithmic suggestions; our quiz develops the user’s individual dining identity and leads discovery based on their tastes. Each profile produces a map that shows restaurant recommendations across major U.S. foodie hubs: Miami, New York, Dallas, Las Vegas, and Los Angeles. Finding your next bite should be taste‑based, intuitive, engaging, and meaningful. The site presents six distinct taste profiles, each represented by a dedicated interactive map built in Mapbox. Together, the profiles and maps illustrate how personalized frameworks can bridge the gap between user preference and spatial exploration.
