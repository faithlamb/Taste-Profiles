Analysis & Findings

When we analyzed the data, a few clear patterns showed why taste-profile-based discovery matters. For example, trend-driven restaurants were heavily concentrated in dense urban areas, especially neighborhoods known for nightlife, design, and social buzz. On the other hand, Comfort Seekers and Local Loyalists showed a completely different pattern. Their restaurants were more spread out and often located in quieter, more community-focused neighborhoods. We also saw an interesting contrast between Culinary Purists and Adventurous Explorers. Purist restaurants tended to be located in more established fine-dining areas, while Adventurous Explorer spots popped up in more eclectic or up-and-coming food scenes. These patterns highlight how different taste profiles connect not just to preferences, but to specific parts of a city’s food culture. Overall, mapping these discoveries communicated the unique identities clearly through spatial patterns. It reinforced the idea that taste profiles can genuinely shape how people discover restaurants and make the process feel more personal and engaging.
