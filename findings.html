Analysis & Findings

Our analysis revealed several patterns that demonstrate the value of taste‑profile‑based restaurant discovery. First, we observed that trend‑chasing restaurants tend to cluster in dense urban centers, particularly in neighborhoods known for nightlife, design culture, or high social visibility. This suggests that the Trend Chaser profile aligns strongly with environments where novelty and aesthetics are prioritized. In contrast, Comfort Seeker and Local Loyalist restaurants were more geographically dispersed, often located in quieter neighborhoods or areas with strong community identity. These patterns reflect the emotional and experiential motivations behind these profiles, comfort‑driven diners gravitate toward warm, familiar spaces rather than high‑traffic hotspots. The Culinary Purist and Adventurous Explorer profiles showed another interesting contrast. Purist restaurants tended to be located in established fine‑dining districts, while Adventurous Explorer restaurants appeared in more eclectic or emerging culinary zones. This distinction highlights how taste profiles can reveal not only user preferences but also the cultural geography of a city’s dining landscape. Across all profiles, mapping the recommendations made the differences between personas more visible and intuitive. The spatial patterns reinforced the idea that taste profiles can meaningfully shape how users navigate restaurant discovery, offering a more personalized and engaging experience than traditional list‑based tools.
