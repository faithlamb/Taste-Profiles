Conclusion

This project demonstrated that personalizing the hunt for new restaurants by unique taste profiles can significantly enhance user experience. Through curated personas and interactive geospatial visualizations, we showed how users can explore restaurants in a way that is intuitive, relevant, and reflective of their preferences. The maps make abstract taste profiles tangible, revealing spatial patterns within urban food culture. Our findings suggest that integrating taste profiles into restaurant discovery platforms could reduce decision fatigue, increase user satisfaction, and create more meaningful connections between eaters and their environments. At the same time, the project raises new questions: How might these profiles evolve with user behavior over time? Could AI personalize maps dynamically moving forward? What additional data, such as price, ambiance, or dietary needs, could refine the experience? By bridging persona‑based design with interactive mapping, our project contributes to a growing conversation about personalization in digital tools. It highlights the potential for restaurant discovery systems to become more human‑centered, expressive, and engaging through the thoughtful use of taste profiles.
